The ordinary engine Resume API rebuilds the team and accepts option overrides ([conductor.js](../../../../../src/conductor.js#L172)). Persist and enforce the original candidate-to-destination binding at both checks. Handle existing name-only provenance conservatively.
3. **P1 — A LocalAI CLI override can contradict the private endpoint.** [knowledge/run.js](../../../../../src/knowledge/run.js#L37) trusts `LocalAgentAdapter.baseUrl`; [localAgent.js](../../../../../src/adapters/localAgent.js#L57) forwards user overrides to Codex after the nominal provider selection.
