# Meeting notes: small change to the notes app

Staff asked for one small thing. Under each note's title, show when the note was last edited, in the form
"Last edited 3 March 2026".

Example: a note that was last edited on 3 March 2026 shows "Last edited 3 March 2026" under its title.

Nothing else changes. No new settings, no new pages, and the way notes are saved stays the same.
