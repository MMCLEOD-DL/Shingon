# Changelog

## [Unreleased]
- If an update's settings write fails after replacing `.env`, the updater restores and checks the previous settings before restarting the app, without restoring the database or uploaded files unnecessarily. If recovery fails, it keeps the failed-update journal and backup reference for operator recovery, even when the failure was recorded in the database.
- A PDF receipt of more than three pages is now refused with "This PDF has more than 3 pages, so it can't be read automatically. Please type the details." Before, only its first three pages were read and the reading said nothing of the rest, so a total or date on a later page (a corrected total, say) was lost and an earlier one could be proposed with full confidence.

## [0.2.0] - 2026-10-29
- **Upgrading an app from 0.1.0** (details in BUILDING-ON, "Upgrading an app from v0.1.0 to v0.2.0"): an app that has made a release on 0.1.0 has a `foundation.json` its own releases overwrote, so after merging restore it with `git checkout v0.2.0 -- foundation.json` and commit.
- **Upgrading an app from 0.1.0:** pages now come from `routeObjectsFor(modules)` (`apps/web/src/routes.tsx`) and `ALL_APP_PATHS` (`apps/web/src/appPaths.ts`); move any page added to 0.1.0's `routeObjects` or `APP_PATHS` arrays into them. `sendReport` takes the config as its second argument.
- Optional modules: app-profile.json chooses modules and the data policy; money helpers in whole pence.
- Node 22.13 or later is now needed (`engines.node` `>=22.13`), because the PDF reader the receipt module uses requires it.
- Receipt reading on the box itself: Tesseract (tesseract.js, model read from disk, never downloaded), the text of PDF receipts, a corpus of fictional rendered receipts, and `npm run test:ocr` with an accuracy report in `reports/`.
- Each PDF receipt is read in a short-lived process of its own, held to 320 MB of memory; pictures over 25 megapixels are refused before they are read.
- Receipt reading in the cloud (Azure Document Intelligence `prebuilt-receipt`), only when app-profile.json's data policy allows `azure-document-intelligence`, `OCR_AZURE_ENDPOINT` is a plain `https://<name>.cognitiveservices.azure.com` address, the region fits the residency and `.env` sets `OCR_ENGINE=azure`; anything else stops the app at start with a plain sentence and nothing is sent. If the cloud can't be reached the receipt is read on the box instead. `OCR_AZURE_REGION` is the region the operator declares for the Azure resource; the app does not detect it, so the operator must check it matches the resource. Tested only against a stand-in; the field names are to be checked against the live service before first real use.
- Receipt readings on the server (when app-profile.json lists `ocr`): `POST /api/files/:id/reading` asks for a reading of an uploaded photo or PDF, a background queue reads it (`OCR_CONCURRENCY` at once, picking up any left waiting after a restart), `GET /api/readings/:id` shows the proposal with a confidence per field, and `POST /api/readings/:id/confirm` records what a person confirmed and which fields they corrected (names only in the audit log). A reading follows its file's rules, is deleted with it, and keeps no recognised text. With `ocr` off, no reading route, queue or reader exists. Demo data adds two receipts for the first Operations member.
- Receipt pages on the phone (when app-profile.json lists `ocr`): Read a receipt (`/receipts/new`, Take a photo or Choose a file) and Check the details (`/receipts/:id`), with a Receipts menu entry. The browser turns photos upright from their EXIF orientation and sends them as JPEG at most 2000 px on the long side; if it can't (an old browser, HEIC on Chrome) the original goes up and the reading says if it couldn't be read. `ReceiptReadingFields` is the confirm-or-correct pattern: fields below 0.85 confidence say "Please check", and nothing is saved until Confirm. `FileDropzone` gains an optional `prepare` step. `npm run test:e2e` builds with every module on (`HF_ALL_MODULES=1`).
- Money reports: `buildMoneyReport` (in `@hf/shared`) checks every cell and works out every subtotal and total once, in whole pence, and `formatCell` shows a cell as people read it (`−£5.00`, `12/03/2026`). The spreadsheet export (`@hf/xlsx-export`, write-excel-file) only lays the report out: totals are written as values, never formulas; money is stored as pounds with a `£#,##0.00` format, dates as real dates, and all text as text, so a merchant like `=HYPERLINK(...)` is never run as a formula. `sendReport` sends a report as a download with `Cache-Control: private, no-store` and `nosniff`. Pinned by worked examples whose totals were worked out by hand.
- PDF export of money reports (`@hf/pdf-export`, pdfkit 0.20.2): `renderPdf` lays the same report out on A4 pages (a header with the column labels on every page, money right-aligned, long text wrapped within its column, a row never split across pages, subtotals after each group, the total once at the end, `Generated …` and `Page N of M` in every footer) and never calculates. It uses only the standard Helvetica fonts, so `toWinAnsi` prints any character outside Windows-1252 as `?` (an emoji is one `?`) and the minus sign as a hyphen (`-£5.00`). Report text is only ever drawn as text, never as a link or action. The same report always gives the same file. `sendReport(..., 'pdf', ...)` sends it as `application/pdf`. `licence-exceptions.json` records png-js 2.0.0 (pdfkit's PNG reader, MIT by its LICENSE file but with no licence field), approved by the operator on 2026-10-28.
- Money reports keep `generatedAt`; spreadsheet money shows a negative as `−£5.00`; download names are at most 120 characters, extension included.
- Scheduled app jobs (when app-profile.json lists `scheduled-jobs`): a client feature declares a job with `registerAppJob` (`@hf/server/jobs`; a name, a label, a five-part cron schedule in UK time and `catchUp: 'once'` or `'skip'`) and adds its file to `apps/server/src/jobs/index.ts`. The ops daemon runs them, never the app, so they stop during an update and never run twice when the app restarts. Each run is claimed in the new `job_runs` table by job and due time, so a due time runs at most once, even across ops restarts; after the box was off, a `'once'` job runs once for the latest missed due time, not once per missed slot. A job gets the database and a log only, and 30 minutes per run. A failure, a run past its time limit, a due run skipped because the last one is still going (alerted once) and a run cut short by an ops stop (never run again, reported once at the next start) are alerted as `the scheduled job "<label>" failed` with one line. Ops starts its own jobs first and catches up in the background, so a hung app job never holds up backups or the monitor. `ops jobs` lists each job with its last run. Ops now reads app-profile.json too (`APP_PROFILE` overrides the path, as for the app); a bad file switches the app jobs off with a warning and backups carry on.
- An app's release no longer overwrites foundation.json; only the foundation's own release stamps it.
- ops update --json prints the outcome as one line of JSON, for scripts.
- New ops commands: status [--json], set-env (from standard input, known settings only) and test-alert.
- The smoke test takes HF_SMOKE_DIR and HF_SMOKE_PROJECT, and kills the database, app and ops without warning to prove they recover.
- The app and ops images carry app-profile.json and the optional modules (built before the server), and the app image ships the PDF reader's child process and the demo receipts. The app image leaves out what it never loads (pdfjs-dist's native canvas and browser builds, the browser copies of the Tesseract engines, the legacy English model and source maps).
- OCR settings for a box: `.env.example` and `ops init` write `OCR_ENGINE=local` and `OCR_CONCURRENCY=1`, with `OCR_TIMEOUT_MS` and the three `OCR_AZURE_*` lines commented out (cloud OCR only when the data policy allows it); `compose.yaml` passes them to the app only, and a stop waits 70 seconds so a reading can finish; `ops set-env` accepts the six `OCR_*` keys.
- App jobs get `ctx.signal`, aborted when ops stops waiting (30 minutes) or begins to stop; once ops is stopping a job failure is only logged, not alerted; a run the timer holds back is reported as skipped and never started.
- `deploy/smoke/ocr-modest-box.sh` checks local OCR in the app image on a 2-CPU, 2 GB box with no network; the measured figures are in `deploy/smoke/OCR-RESULT.md`.
- A reading still being read can be confirmed (after 90 seconds the Check the details page invites typing): every field counts as corrected, and the queue finishing later never changes a confirmed reading. The queue tries its start-up load again (1, 2, 5, 10 and 30 seconds) if the database is not ready yet.
- `reattachFile(tx, actor, fileId, from, to)` moves a file from one record to another (a receipt photo from its reading to the expense), in the caller's transaction: the row is locked, only the owner or an admin may (others get 404) and the file must still be attached to `from` (otherwise 409). `attachFile` still never moves a file.
- `sendReport` now takes the config: `sendReport(reply, config, report, format, baseName)`. It answers 404 when the module for that format (`pdf-export` or `xlsx-export`) is off, and makes one export at a time (others wait their turn). Register export routes inside `moduleOn` (BUILDING-ON).
- Cloud readings are checked harder: an Azure total or date is at most 0.8 (so "Please check") unless the receipt's lines read the same value, and an Azure merchant is always at most 0.8. A cloud fall-back caused by a 401 or 403 is logged as "the key or endpoint is wrong" rather than "could not be reached".
- With the `ocr` module off, the app no longer asks for the `OCR_AZURE_*` settings even if `.env` says `OCR_ENGINE=azure`.
